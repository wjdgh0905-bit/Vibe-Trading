About

About Melange

Melange builds custom pieces around a consistent wave motion and blue color palette — the throughline across every design.

Every design is drawn to fit the shape and movement of its placement, not fitted to a fixed template. Reference images are a starting point, not something to copy — each piece is redrawn into Melange's own line work and color.

Style & Process

How each piece is made

Style

  • Recurring subjects: dragon, snake, phoenix, koi, whale, flowers, clouds, lightning, pattern
  • Built around wave motion and a blue color palette
  • Adapted to the shape and movement of the placement
  • Reference is reworked, not copied

Process

  • Stencil and freehand are both used, depending on the piece
  • Base elements are prepared ahead of time
  • Sections that follow the body's flow are drawn freehand on the day
  • Final design and any changes are confirmed together before starting

Booking

Booking Information

By appointment only. No walk-ins. Every booking starts with a short consultation.

Prefer to reach out directly? Instagram  ·  Email

What to include in your first message

  • Name
  • City
  • Tattoo idea or preferred design
  • Placement
  • Approximate size in cm
  • Preferred date
  • A clear photo of the placement area

How it works

  1. Send the details above via Instagram DM or email.
  2. Melange reviews your message and replies with size, difficulty, estimated time, and price.
  3. A deposit secures your date — spots are confirmed in the order deposits are received.
  4. The deposit is deducted from the final price and is non-refundable.
  5. Your design is created after the booking is confirmed, not before.
  6. Final design and any changes are reviewed together in person before the tattoo starts.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

No. All work is by appointment only.

Where are you based?

Melange travels as a guest artist across South Korea, Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom, and Europe. Exact cities and dates are posted on Instagram.

How do I get the exact studio address?

It's sent directly once your booking is confirmed.

What should I include in my first message?

Your name, city, tattoo idea or reference, placement, approximate size in cm, preferred date, and a clear photo of the placement area.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on size, placement, and difficulty. You'll get an estimate after sending your inquiry.

Is the deposit refundable?

No. The deposit secures your date and is deducted from the final price.

Can I see the design before the appointment?

Designs are made after booking is confirmed. Base elements may be prepared ahead of time, and parts that follow the body's flow are drawn freehand on the day. The final design is confirmed with you in person before starting.
