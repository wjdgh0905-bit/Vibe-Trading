About

About Melange

Melange is a solo tattoo artist. No team, no other artists.

Work covers a wide range of subjects, reworked through Melange’s own wave-flow and blue-color signature.

Core motifs: dragon, snake, phoenix, koi, whale, flowers, clouds, lightning, patterns.

Each design is built around the shape and movement of the body part being tattooed.

Melange works in both stencil and freehand, depending on the design.

Other artists’ work is never copied directly. Every design is reworked into Melange’s own style.

Booking Information

Booking Information

Appointment only. No walk-ins. Consultation required before booking.

What to include in your inquiry

  • Name
  • City
  • Tattoo idea or preferred design
  • Placement
  • Approximate size in cm
  • Preferred date
  • Clear photo of the placement

How it works

  1. Melange reviews the inquiry and sends size, difficulty, estimated time, and price.
  2. Bookings are confirmed in the order deposits are paid.
  3. The deposit is deducted from the final price. The deposit is non-refundable.
  4. Design work starts only after the booking is confirmed.
  5. Some designs are prepared in advance; parts that need to follow the body’s flow are drawn freehand on the day.
  6. Final design and any changes are confirmed with the client in person before tattooing.
