How your information is used

This page explains what Melange Tattoo (“Melange”, “we”) collects through melangetattoo.ink and while planning your tattoo, and what happens to it. Effective 30 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Melange Tattoo is a solo tattoo artist based in South Korea who also works at guest spots abroad. Melange decides how your information is used and is responsible for it. Contact: melange.tattoo@gmail.com or @melange.tattoo on Instagram.

3. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don’t turn into a booking: deleted 1 year after our last message.
  • Clients’ booking notes: kept 3 years after your last session (for touch-ups and future pieces).
  • Deposit records: kept 5 years, as Korean tax law requires.
  • Melbourne model applications that aren’t chosen: deleted by 31 December 2026.
  • Posted photos and video: kept while posted; taken down if you ask.

When the time is up we delete it from Formspree, email (including trash) and our notes, and shred any paper.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, ask us to stop using it, and withdraw your photo and filming agreement at any time. Email melange.tattoo@gmail.com or DM @melange.tattoo on Instagram. We’ll handle it and reply within 10 days. Someone you authorise can ask for you.

  • When you ask us to delete, we remove it from Formspree, our email and notes, and delete our side of the Instagram chat (your side stays in Instagram). Deposit records we must keep for tax are kept only for that period.
  • Posted photos and video come down on request.
  • We can’t tell who you are from Analytics or Meta reports, but Meta may connect visit data to your Instagram or Facebook account if you’re logged in. You can block or clear it as described in 5.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also contact a privacy regulator: in Korea, the Personal Information Protection Commission’s report centre (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr); in Australia, the OAIC (www.oaic.gov.au); in the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk).

7. Children

Melange only tattoos adults, so we don’t knowingly collect information from children. If we learn an inquiry came from a minor, we decline it and delete it.

8. Keeping it safe

Only Melange can access the accounts that receive your information (email, Formspree, Netlify, Instagram), which are protected with a password and two-step verification. Old information is deleted regularly.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date. 30 October 2026: rewritten to add retention periods, legal bases, overseas services, cookies and how to make requests (previous version: October 2026).
